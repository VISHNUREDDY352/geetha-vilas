📖 How to Use the Admin Panel

1
Open https://geetha-vilas.vercel.app/admin.html on your phone or computer and enter the password.
2
Toggle items ON/OFF — flip the green switch next to any item. It auto-saves to the cloud in 1.5 seconds. Customers see the change instantly.
3
Add new item — click the ➕ Add Item button, choose category, enter name & price, click Add.
4
Edit price — click the ✏️ pencil icon next to any item's price, type new price, press Enter or ✓.
5
Remove item — click the 🗑 delete button. Removed items stay off permanently (not restored at midnight).
6
🔄 Reset All — turns all items back to Available. Also happens automatically every midnight.
